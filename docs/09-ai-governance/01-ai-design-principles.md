# AI Design Principles

## 1. Design objective

Create a beautiful, calm, premium, Arabic-first financial product for the Environment Fund. Beauty must come from hierarchy, proportion, spacing, typography, data clarity, and restrained brand expression—not decoration.

## 2. Non-negotiable principles

1. Read the relevant documents before designing or coding.
2. Do not invent business rules, statuses, fields, roles, approvals, screens, or integrations.
3. Do not redesign the Environment Fund identity.
4. Do not imitate generic admin, CRM, fintech, or SaaS templates.
5. Use Arabic RTL as the default product experience.
6. Keep financial values, workflow decisions, deadlines, and next actions visually dominant.
7. Use progressive disclosure instead of placing all information on one surface.
8. Prefer fewer, stronger visual sections over many small cards.
9. Preserve generous negative space and consistent alignment.
10. Use color as a supporting signal, never as the only communication method.

## 3. Beauty standard

A screen is visually acceptable only when it demonstrates:

- a clear primary focal point;
- disciplined spacing and alignment;
- consistent component proportions;
- restrained use of borders, shadows, and color;
- readable financial data;
- natural Arabic content and RTL behavior;
- strong visual grouping without excessive containers;
- clear distinction between information, decision, and action.

## 4. Prohibited patterns

Do not use:

- glassmorphism;
- neon or unapproved colors;
- decorative gradients;
- heavy drop shadows;
- oversized rounded cards everywhere;
- dense walls of KPI cards;
- random icons or illustrations;
- patterns behind tables, forms, charts, or long text;
- green as the dominant interface color;
- horizontal layouts copied directly from LTR products without RTL redesign;
- placeholder text such as lorem ipsum;
- fake complexity intended only to make the prototype look busy.

## 5. Decision-oriented design

Every operational screen must answer, where applicable:

- What is this item?
- What is its financial significance?
- What is its current status?
- Who is responsible now?
- What happened previously?
- What must happen next?
- Is there a deadline, exception, or risk?

## 6. AI design behavior

Before implementation, the AI agent must summarize:

- the user goal;
- the source documents used;
- the intended page hierarchy;
- reusable components required;
- responsive adaptations;
- unresolved assumptions.

The agent must not start coding a page until this internal design plan is coherent and traceable to repository documentation.
