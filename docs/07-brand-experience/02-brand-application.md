# Brand Application

## Logo usage

Use `ef-logo-horizontal-blue.svg` in the primary application shell on light surfaces. Use `ef-logo-horizontal-white.svg` only on approved dark navy or blue surfaces. Reserve the full KSA lockup for formal entry, about, or executive contexts where the complete institutional identity is required.

The symbol alone may be used for the favicon, compact navigation state, or subtle watermark. It must not replace the full logo in the standard expanded shell.

Never recolor, distort, rotate, stretch, rebuild, or place the logo over a busy pattern.

## Application shell

The shell should express the identity through structure rather than decoration:

- a clean light workspace;
- primary blue for selected navigation and primary actions;
- navy for strongest text and selected executive surfaces;
- restrained warm-grey sections;
- logo placed in a stable, uncluttered area;
- consistent RTL navigation and content alignment.

Do not place the logo inside every page header, card, report widget, or modal.

## Pattern usage

Approved uses:

- sign-in or prototype entry screen;
- restrained dashboard welcome/header region;
- empty states with significant unused space;
- about/help screen;
- formal confirmation or completion moment;
- narrow side or corner accent.

Prohibited uses:

- behind tables, charts, filters, forms, attachments, or workflow controls;
- on every page;
- in multiple corners at once;
- at high opacity;
- as a repeated tile;
- where it competes with financial data.

## Radial graphic

The radial element is a high-impact brand device. Use it more sparingly than the pattern. Suitable locations include the entry screen, an executive overview header, a major empty state, or a formal prototype cover.

It must remain cropped intentionally, low in visual dominance, and separated from primary text.

## Page-level brand intensity

| Page type | Brand intensity |
|---|---|
| Sign-in / prototype entry | high but controlled |
| Dashboard | medium |
| My Tasks | low |
| Investment Request list | low |
| Transaction workspace | very low; task clarity first |
| Deposit Portfolio | low |
| Reports | low to medium through chart palette |
| Settings | minimal |
| Empty / completion state | medium |

## Cards and surfaces

Cards use white or approved soft-neutral surfaces, subtle borders, limited elevation, and moderate radii. Identity should come from typography, spacing, selected accents, and meaningful states—not from making every card blue or patterned.

## Primary actions

Primary actions use Environment Fund primary blue. Destructive actions use the product danger semantic token. Success actions do not automatically become green buttons.

## Charts

Charts use approved brand colors with controlled variation. Avoid rainbow palettes. Every chart must provide direct labels, a legend where required, meaningful ordering, accessible contrast, and a clear decision purpose.

## Dark surfaces

Use dark navy or primary-blue surfaces only for deliberately prominent areas. Keep them compact. Use white text and validate contrast. Do not turn the entire operational application into a dark theme for the prototype.

## Photography and illustration

The prototype should not depend on photography. When illustrations are required, use abstract institutional geometry derived from approved EF patterns and radial language. Avoid generic leaves, trees, globes, coins, handshakes, or stock banking imagery.
