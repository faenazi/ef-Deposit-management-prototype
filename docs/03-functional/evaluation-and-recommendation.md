# Evaluation and Recommendation

## Purpose

Support a documented and defensible comparison of received bank offers and record the specialist's recommendation.

## Evaluation Dimensions

The prototype must support assessment of:

- Offered rate.
- Expected return.
- Tenor suitability.
- Value and maturity dates.
- Liquidity impact.
- Early-break terms.
- Offer validity.
- Portfolio concentration by bank.
- Existing exposure to the bank.
- Historical relationship notes.
- Completeness and compliance of the offer.

## Evaluation Table

The comparison view must show all shortlisted offers in a consistent structure and allow the user to understand why one offer is preferred.

## Recommendation Record

Capture:

- Recommended bank.
- Recommended offer.
- Recommended amount.
- Recommended tenor.
- Expected return.
- Business rationale.
- Key risks or considerations.
- Alternatives considered.
- Specialist name.
- Recommendation date.

## Rules

- The recommended offer must be valid and complete.
- The recommendation must include a written rationale.
- A lower-rate offer may be recommended when justified by liquidity, concentration, break terms, or other documented considerations.
- The recommendation becomes read-only after submission, unless the request is returned to the specialist.
- Changes after return must be reflected in activity history.

## UX Requirements

- Do not hide important comparison dimensions behind multiple dialogs.
- Clearly separate objective offer facts from specialist judgment.
- Use restrained visual emphasis for the recommended offer.
- Show portfolio concentration context near the decision, not on a separate unrelated page.
