# Users and Personas

## 1. Deposit Specialist

Primary operational user responsible for preparing investment requests and following them through execution and activation.

### Goals

- Prepare complete and accurate investment requests.
- Compare bank offers and document the recommendation.
- Resolve returned items quickly.
- Complete winning-bank and activation information.

### Main Actions

- Create, edit, save, delete, and submit drafts.
- Record liquidity information, RFQs, offers, evaluation, and attachments.
- Respond to return comments.
- Complete winning bank information.
- Confirm deposit activation.

## 2. General Manager of Treasury

First approval authority for all submitted investment requests.

### Goals

- Validate the investment rationale, liquidity, offers, and recommendation.
- Approve, reject, or return requests with clear reasons.
- Monitor pending approvals and treasury workload.

### Main Actions

- Review complete request files.
- Approve requests up to and including SAR 100,000,000.
- Approve and forward requests above SAR 100,000,000.
- Return requests to the Deposit Specialist.

## 3. Executive Director of Investment and Treasury Sector

Executive approval authority for requests above SAR 100,000,000.

### Goals

- Review material investment decisions.
- Confirm strategic and risk considerations.
- Preserve the management approval hierarchy.

### Main Actions

- Approve, reject, or return eligible requests.
- View approval history and decision evidence.

## 4. Investment Support Specialist

Control and completeness reviewer after investment approval and winning-bank completion.

### Goals

- Confirm approvals, offer evidence, beneficiary information, and mandatory documents.
- Prevent incomplete requests from reaching Finance.

### Main Actions

- Complete a structured review checklist.
- Approve, reject, or return to the Deposit Specialists group.

## 5. Finance Specialist

Financial control reviewer with limited access to required financial and beneficiary information.

### Goals

- Validate payment readiness and beneficiary details.
- Approve the request for accounting execution.

### Main Actions

- Review limited relevant information.
- Approve, reject, or return to Investment Support.

## 6. Accounting Specialist

Executes the transfer and records execution evidence.

### Goals

- Execute the approved transfer accurately.
- Capture reference numbers, dates, and supporting evidence.

### Main Actions

- Record transfer execution.
- Attach transfer evidence.
- Record or simulate the accounting entry reference.
- Return the request to Finance when required.

## 7. System Administrator

Maintains prototype reference data and demonstration settings.

### Main Actions

- Manage mock users, banks, statuses, thresholds, and demo scenarios.
- Reset prototype data.

The administrator must not bypass the business workflow during normal demonstrations.

## 8. Read-only User

Stakeholder or auditor with view-only access.

### Main Actions

- View authorized requests, deposits, reports, attachments, and activity history.
- Cannot create, edit, approve, return, reject, execute, or activate.

## Persona Experience Rule

Switching the active prototype user must change the dashboard, task queue, visible information, editable sections, and available actions. Role switching must demonstrate genuine permission differences rather than merely changing the displayed user name.
