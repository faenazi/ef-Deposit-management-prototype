# Business Glossary

## Investment Request

The transaction file prepared to propose placing available liquidity into a single bank deposit. One approved and executed investment request creates one active deposit.

## Deposit

A time-bound investment placed with one bank under an agreed amount, rate, tenor, start date, and maturity date.

## Draft

The preparation state before first submission. The request remains Draft while the specialist progressively completes all required sections.

## Returned for Completion

A previously submitted request returned to an earlier responsible role for correction or additional information. It is not treated as a new Draft and retains its full history.

## RFQ

Request for Quotation sent to selected banks to obtain deposit offers.

## Bank Offer

A bank response containing proposed rate, tenor, dates, amount, validity, conditions, and related documents.

## Winning Offer

The approved offer selected after evaluation and recommendation.

## Expected Return

The estimated financial return calculated from the amount, annual rate, and tenor. The canonical prototype formula is `principal × annualRate × tenorDays ÷ 360` on a 360-day banking basis (see `docs/02-business/business-rules.md`).

## Value Date

The deposit start date on which invested funds begin earning return. User-facing Arabic label: `تاريخ بدء الوديعة`. Code identifier: `valueDate`.

## Tenor

The agreed investment duration, such as 2, 3, 6, 9, or 12 months.

## Maturity Date

The date on which the deposit principal and applicable return become due.

## Early Break

Termination of a deposit before its agreed maturity date, subject to bank terms and possible return reduction or penalties.

## Reinvestment

Creating a new investment decision using proceeds from a matured deposit. Reinvestment must preserve a link to the source deposit but creates a new investment request and deposit.

## Deposit Activation

The final confirmation that the bank has established the deposit after transfer execution. Activation converts the completed request into an active portfolio record.

## Investment Support Review

The control review performed after investment approval and completion of winning-bank information.

## Finance Review

The financial readiness and beneficiary validation performed before accounting execution.

## Accounting Execution

The transfer execution stage in which payment and accounting references are recorded and evidence is attached.

## Current Owner

The user or role group currently responsible for the next required action.

## Task

A role-specific actionable item generated by a workflow stage, return action, or maturity event.

## Activity History

The immutable chronological record of creation, edits, submissions, approvals, returns, rejections, attachments, and execution events.
